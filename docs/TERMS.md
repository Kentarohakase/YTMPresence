## What YTMPresence is

YTMPresence shows the YouTube Music track you are playing as Discord Rich Presence. The browser extension reads the playback state on `music.youtube.com` and sends it to the companion app on your computer, which passes it to the Discord desktop app. The [privacy notice](EXTENSION_PRIVACY.md) describes which data this involves and where it goes. YTMPresence is free to use and has no accounts.
